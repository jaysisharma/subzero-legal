Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

SubZero ("we", "the app") helps you track your subscriptions and cancel the ones you don't use. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

SubZero never asks for your bank, card, UPI, SMS or email-inbox access.

1. Who we are

SubZero is developed by Jaysi Sharma.
Contact: socialmediahandle2026@gmail.com

2. Data we collect

DataWhyWhere it is stored

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, SMS, bank statements or payment credentials. We do not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and we do not show ads.

3. Auto-fill from a receipt

If you tap "Auto-Fill from Receipt or SMS", the app reads the text currently on your clipboard (for example a payment message you copied) to pre-fill the form. This happens only when you tap it, the text is processed on your device, and it is not sent to us or stored.

4. In-app guided cancellation

When you use "Cancel inside SubZero", the service's own website opens inside the app. You sign in directly with that service; SubZero never sees or stores your password. The app reads the visible page text only to highlight likely cancel buttons and to detect a cancellation confirmation; that text is processed on your device and is not sent to us. Website login cookies stay on your device and are deleted when you sign out of SubZero or delete your account.

5. Notifications and biometrics

Billing reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Biometric unlock uses your device's built-in fingerprint / face / passcode system; SubZero never receives your biometric data.

6. How we use data

Only to provide the app's features: syncing your ledger across sign-ins, reminders, insights and savings. We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

7. Sharing

Data is processed by Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) as our service provider, under Google's terms. We share data with no one else unless required by law.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest by Google Cloud. Database rules ensure each account can read and write only its own subscriptions.

9. Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account and all your subscription data at any time in Settings → Delete Account. This is immediate and permanent. You can also request deletion without the app. Data on your device is removed when you uninstall the app.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the EU/UK GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, and to withdraw consent. You can export your subscriptions in Settings → Export to CSV, delete your account in Settings, or contact us at socialmediahandle2026@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

11. Children

SubZero is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their data.

12. Changes

We will post updates to this policy on this page and change the "Last updated" date.
