Delete your SubZero account

SubZero, developed by Jaysi Sharma

Delete it in the app (instant)

  1. Open SubZero and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile (bottom right).
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Enter your password and tap Delete.

Your account and all its data are removed immediately.

Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, email socialmediahandle2026@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my SubZero account". We will confirm it is your account, delete it within 30 days, and reply when it is done.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in account (email address and password) in Firebase Authentication.
  • Every subscription you added, with its amounts, dates, usage answers and cancellation history.

What is kept

Nothing. We keep no copy of your account or subscriptions after deletion. Settings stored only on your phone (currency, budget, lock and incognito preferences) are removed when you uninstall the app.
